Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

That's Suber ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Noted iOS application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Noted is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect as little data as possible.

Data stored on your device: All notes you create in Noted are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

We do not collect:

  • Personal information such as your name or email address
  • The content of your notes
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers

How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect personal information, we do not use or share your personal data in any way. Your notes remain entirely private and on your device.

Third Party Services

Noted does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not share any data with third parties.

Data Security

All data created in Noted is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security features including passcode, Face ID, and Touch ID. We have no access to this data.

Children's Privacy

Noted does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from any users, no special handling is required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@thatssuber.com.
